# Care Guide

## Storage
Store folded in breathable muslin with neem leaves to deter insects. Never store in plastic bags — silk needs to breathe. Keep in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

## Washing silk and zari
Dry clean only. Spot-clean stains immediately with a soft cloth.

## Cotton-silk blends
Gentle hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Do not wring.

## Ironing
Iron on low heat with a cloth barrier. Never iron directly on zari.
