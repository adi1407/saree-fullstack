# FAQ — Returns

## What is your return policy?
Unworn sarees with original tags may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Custom or altered pieces are not eligible.

## Can I exchange for a different saree?
Yes — exchanges are processed as a return and new order. Contact us within 7 days to initiate. Exchange for a different saree of equal or greater value within 14 days is also available subject to availability.

## How do returns work?
Email care@aadiora.com with your order number. We arrange pickup in metro cities; elsewhere, secure courier at our cost for approved returns. Approved refunds process within 7–10 business days to your original payment method via Razorpay.
